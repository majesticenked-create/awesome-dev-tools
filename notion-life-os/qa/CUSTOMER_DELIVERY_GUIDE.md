## 1. What you get

LifeOS - All-in-One Life Planner is a Notion template that brings your day, goals, habits, money, home and life admin into one calm workspace.

Two editions are included:

- **Example Edition:** filled with realistic sample data so you can see how everything connects. Best if you like to learn by looking.
- **Blank Edition:** the same system with the sample data removed (your eight life areas and budget categories are kept as a starting point). Best if you want a clean start.

Inside: a Hub dashboard, Start Here guide, Today, Plan, Mind, Body & Home, Money and Life Admin pages, all powered by 21 linked databases.

## 5. Troubleshooting

**I clicked Duplicate but nothing happened.** Make sure you are signed in to Notion, then open the link again.

**A view is empty.** Most views are filtered, for example "due today". Empty usually means nothing matches yet. Add an entry, or click the view name to see its filter.

**A progress bar says "No tasks linked yet".** Link at least one task (or project) to the goal or project.

**An expense is not in my budget.** It needs a Category, a Date in the current month and Type set to Expense.

**The budget reset to zero.** Budgets count the current calendar month and start fresh on the 1st. Older transactions are still in Transactions.

**Notion asks me to upgrade when I open Money.** See the Charts note in section 4.

**I deleted something by mistake.** Open Trash in the Notion sidebar and restore it, or press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac) straight away.
