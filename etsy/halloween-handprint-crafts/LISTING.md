## FAQ entries (Etsy shop FAQ)

- **What paper should I use?** White cardstock (65–110 lb / 176–300 gsm) holds paint best. Regular printer paper works too — use a little less paint.
- **What paint works best?** Washable tempera or finger paint. Brush it on in a thin coat instead of dipping.
- **Can I use these in my classroom?** Yes! One purchase covers one teacher's class.
- **How do I type the names?** Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) and click the line next to *name*, *age* or *year*.
